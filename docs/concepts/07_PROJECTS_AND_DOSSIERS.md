# Projects and dossiers

Status: exploratory rationale, captured 2026-10-15; no new implementation or
dependency decision. See the [concepts guide](README.md) for authority and provenance.

## Idea and lesson

A dossier brings together scoped evidence and reviewed assertions about a project while retaining source identity and uncertainty.

## Illustrative use

A fictional loan dossier can connect an appraisal, a commitment and a term sheet. Similar names may still refer to different deals.

## Boundaries and next evidence

Aliases are not permission to merge entities. A cited export is bounded by its contract, current grants and source checks, not proof of complete or current real-world truth.
