# Brain folders

Status: exploratory rationale, captured 2026-10-15; no new implementation or
dependency decision. See the [concepts guide](README.md) for authority and provenance.

## Idea and lesson

A conceptual folder can express an attention view, a query or a proposed command. Those meanings must be explicit before building an interface.

## Illustrative use

A proposed “Review later” view could group evidence without moving any original message. A future drag gesture could propose a preference for review.

## Boundaries and next evidence

Virtual grouping must not imply provider-folder creation, source movement or training consent. Ambiguous gestures need confirmation of meaning; permissions and execution require their own contract.
