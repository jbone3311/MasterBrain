# Knowledge core

Status: exploratory rationale, captured 2026-10-15; no new implementation or
dependency decision. See the [concepts guide](README.md) for authority and provenance.

## Idea and lesson

Preserved evidence and reviewed knowledge serve different purposes. DataCore retains and structures source material; KnowledgeCore represents evidence-backed assertions, relationships and corrections.

## Illustrative use

Two documents can disagree about a maturity date. Keep both assertions and their source/time context instead of silently replacing one with a model summary.

## Boundaries and next evidence

A concept guide cannot define new tables or correction rules. Follow the data model and knowledge contract; keep unknown or disputed facts visible.
