# Learning and rules

Status: exploratory rationale, captured 2026-10-15; no new implementation or
dependency decision. See the [concepts guide](README.md) for authority and provenance.

## Idea and lesson

Corrections should become traceable proposals rather than invisible model changes. Distinguish an assertion correction, a personal preference and an executable rule.

## Illustrative use

Correcting a fictional project alias does not authorize future mail moves. A proposed rule needs its basis, scope, review and reversal behavior.

## Boundaries and next evidence

Do not equate ordinary browsing with approved training or rule execution. Typed validation and policy remain outside the model.
