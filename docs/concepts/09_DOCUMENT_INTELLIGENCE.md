## Idea and lesson

Separate preserving bytes, extracting text/layout, identifying statements, reviewing knowledge and executing actions. Success at one stage does not establish the next.

## Illustrative use

An extracted appraisal table may be searchable while its proposed valuation remains unreviewed. Page evidence and exact quotes make inspection possible.
