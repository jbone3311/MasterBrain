Status: exploratory rationale, captured 2026-10-15; no new implementation or

## Idea and lesson

Periodic review is a proposed way to surface new connections, contradictions and unanswered questions. A discovery is a candidate insight with evidence.

## Illustrative use

A fictional weekly review might show two conflicting dates and ask the owner to resolve them, rather than announce a newly established deadline.
